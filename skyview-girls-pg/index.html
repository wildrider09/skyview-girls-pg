Welcome to SkyView Girls PG

A safe, secure and comfortable home away from home
for students & working women in Dehradun.

SkyView Girls PG offers a warm, family-like environment where you can focus on your studies and career with complete peace of mind. Located in a peaceful, well-connected neighbourhood of Dehradun — close to colleges, coaching centres, hospitals and the main market — our PG combines clean, furnished rooms with nutritious home-cooked meals and round-the-clock security. From high-speed WiFi to daily housekeeping, every detail is taken care of so you always feel right at home.
