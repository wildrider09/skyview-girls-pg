Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what most students, working women and parents ask us.

Who can stay at SkyView Girls PG?

SkyView is a women-only residence for girl students and working professionals. We welcome long-term residents looking for a safe, homely and well-connected place to stay in Dehradun.

What is included in the monthly rent?

Tariffs typically include home-cooked meals, WiFi, electricity, 24-hour water, housekeeping and security. Exact inclusions can vary by room type — please contact us to confirm current pricing and availability.

Are meals provided?

Home-style meals are available through a trusted outside vendor at an additional cost (not included in the room rent). There’s also a clean common kitchen if you prefer to cook your own food. Tell us your dietary preferences and we’ll share the meal options and charges.

How safe is the PG?

Safety is our top priority. We have 24×7 CCTV surveillance, a resident warden, secure entry and a women-only environment, so you and your family can have complete peace of mind.

Is there a security deposit or notice period?

A refundable security deposit usually applies, along with a short notice period before moving out. Please contact us for the exact terms — we keep them simple and transparent.

How do I book a room or visit?

The easiest way is to message us on WhatsApp or call us, or fill in the contact form below. You’re also welcome to drop by in person — find us on Google Maps for directions to our location. We’ll be happy to show you the rooms and facilities.
